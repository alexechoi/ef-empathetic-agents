# Alex and Dad — approved demo memories

This is synthetic hackathon demo data. Every memory below is approved for use
during calls with Alex.

## Encouragement and work

- Before important interviews, Dad told Alex: “Be yourself, breathe, and take
  the questions one at a time.”
- Before Alex's first day at work, Dad said he was already proud, regardless of
  how the day went.
- Before exams, Dad's usual phrase was: “You've done the work. Now go show them
  what you've got.”
- When Alex worried about making a mistake, Dad said: “You don't need the whole
  staircase. Just take the next step.”

## Family routines

- Sunday lunch was a family ritual. Dad usually made roast chicken, crispy
  potatoes, carrots, and gravy. He started the gravy too early and checked it
  repeatedly.
- Dad played a Motown playlist while cooking Sunday lunch and quietly sang the
  wrong lyrics.
- Dad always made tea with a small splash of milk and two sugars. Alex teased
  him that it was closer to dessert than tea.
- After lunch, Dad asked everyone for one good thing that had happened during
  their week.

## Shared interests and places

- Alex and Dad watched Wimbledon together. Their ritual was strawberries during
  the first set and tea during the second.
- Dad taught Alex to serve in tennis by saying: “Throw the ball higher than you
  think, then reach for it.”
- A favourite family trip was a rainy week in Cornwall. Dad insisted on walking
  along the beach, then bought everyone hot chips.
- Dad grew tomatoes in the garden and gave too many of them to neighbours every
  summer.

## Food, music, and humour

- Dad's reliable quick dinner was tomato pasta with garlic, basil, and far too
  much parmesan.
- One of Dad's favourite songs was “Here Comes the Sun.” He played it on bright
  mornings and during long drives.
- Dad's practical joke phrase was: “Measure twice, cut once — then ask where I
  left the tape measure.”
- On birthdays, Dad left a voice message early in the morning, usually before
  the birthday person was awake.

## Answering guidance

per-call context. If a detail is not present, say that it was not included in
the memories Alex saved. Never invent an answer, claim Dad is communicating, or
